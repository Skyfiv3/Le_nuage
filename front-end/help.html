Help Center

Welcome to the Le Nuage Help Center.
Here you can find answers to common questions and learn how to use the platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

💡 How do I create an account?

You can create an account by visiting the Sign Up page.

🔐 I forgot my password, what should I do?

Go to the login page and click on “Forgot password”. You will receive a reset email.

🎬 Why are some movies missing?

Our database depends on external APIs. If a movie is missing, it may not be available yet.

📝 How do I edit my profile?

Go to your profile page and click “Edit profile” to update your information.

🌍 In which countries is Le Nuage available?

Le Nuage is accessible worldwide, although some movie content may vary by region.

📨 How can I contact support?

You can contact us through our Contact page or by email at: support@lenuage.com

Need more help?

If you couldn’t find the answer you were looking for, feel free to reach out anytime.
